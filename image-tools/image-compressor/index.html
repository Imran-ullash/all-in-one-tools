How It Works & Underlying Formula

The image is loaded into an HTML5 Canvas context. The browser uses hardware-accelerated bicubic resampling and native JPEG/WebP quantization to compress the pixel buffer into a compact binary Blob via `canvas.toBlob(type, quality)` without ever sending your image to an external server.

Practical Calculation Example

A high-resolution 4.5 MB photo taken with a smartphone can easily be compressed to under 450 KB (a 90% reduction) with virtually no noticeable difference on screens.

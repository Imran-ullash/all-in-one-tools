How It Works & Underlying Formula

Percentages represent fractions of 100. The calculator employs standard algebraic equations: - Percentage of a value: P = (X / 100) × Y - Percentage share: P = (X / Y) × 100 - Percentage difference: Change = ((Final - Initial) / |Initial|) × 100

Practical Calculation Example

If an item originally costs $80 and is discounted to $60, the percentage decrease is calculated as ((60 - 80) / 80) × 100 = -25%, indicating a 25% discount.

How It Works & Underlying Formula

The date calculator measures the difference between timestamps converted to midnight UTC, avoiding timezone skew and Daylight Saving Time (DST) shift artifacts. Business days are calculated by stepping across each day in the span and excluding Saturday (day 6) and Sunday (day 0).

Practical Calculation Example

From January 1, 2025 to February 15, 2025 is 45 calendar days, equivalent to 6 weeks and 3 days, and contains 33 business days.

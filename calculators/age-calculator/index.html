How It Works & Underlying Formula

The Age Calculator utilizes precise calendar mathematics that accounts for varying month lengths (28, 29, 30, and 31 days) as well as leap years in the Gregorian calendar. The calculation subtracts the birth year, month, and day from the reference date, dynamically borrowing days from the preceding month when day subtraction produces a negative value.

Practical Calculation Example

If you were born on October 15, 1998, and calculate your age as of October 15, 2024, the calculator computes precisely 26 Years, 0 Months, and 0 Days. If calculated on November 20, 2024, the result displays 26 Years, 1 Month, and 5 Days (approximately 9,533 total days).

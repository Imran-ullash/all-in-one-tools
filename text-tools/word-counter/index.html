How It Works & Underlying Formula

The tool uses regex-based boundary tokenization (`\s+` and sentence terminators `[.!?]+`) to accurately tally words and sentences while gracefully filtering empty whitespace sequences. Reading time is estimated using the cognitive standard of 200 words per minute (WPM), while speaking time uses 130 WPM.

Practical Calculation Example

A standard 500-word blog post has an average reading time of 2.5 minutes and a spoken presentation length of approximately 3.8 minutes.
